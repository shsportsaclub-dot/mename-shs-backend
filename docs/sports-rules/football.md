# Football: rules verified for the Match Center (IFAB Laws of the Game 2026/27, effective 1 July 2026)
Source: https://www.theifab.com/news/the-ifab-introduces-further-measures-to-improve-match-flow-and-player-behaviour (read 13 Nov 2026)

Scorer must model
- Law 3: 5 substitutes in 3 windows plus half-time (existing). Senior "A" international friendlies: 8 substitutes, 11 if both teams agree (NEW setting).
- Law 12: a second yellow = red (existing). If advantage is played after a denied obvious goal-scoring opportunity and a goal is scored, the offender is NOT cautioned (NEW check when a goal follows a DOGSO foul).
- Substitution: the player must leave within 10 seconds, else the substitute enters only at the first stoppage after one minute of running clock (NEW note/timer on sub events).
- Injured player assessed on the field must stay off one minute of running clock after the restart (NEW flag).
- Throw-in / goal-kick time-wasting: 5-second visual countdown; unfinished throw-in goes to opponents, delayed goal kick gives opponents a corner (NEW event types: countdown, corner awarded).
- VAR scope: second yellow clearly wrong, mistaken identity, wrongly awarded corner (competition option) (NEW VAR event reasons).
- Law 8: dropped ball goes to the team that would have kept/gained possession (restart label).
- Not approved (do not implement): goalkeeper 8-second change for 2026/27 beyond the 2025 rule, leaving the field in protest, mouth covering.
