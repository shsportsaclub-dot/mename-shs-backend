# Reference research (5 links) — findings

Method: opened in browser, scrolled, screenshots. Aptoide is blocked in the browser; its text was read via fetch.

## 1. Dribbble — "Cricket Scorecard" (Akshay Syal, "Ballebaaz")
- Phone scorer UI, white + red themes.
- Score pill: team code, score/wkts, overs, CRR; chase line "need 12 runs in 6 balls".
- Opposition score line, last-6-balls bead strip (1, W red, 4 blue, wd grey, 0, 6 green).
- Extras line "Extra 12 (B 0, LB 0, WD 0, NB 0, P 0)", Fall of wickets "22-1, 39-2, 60-3".
- Tabs Quick / Advanced; keypad 0 1 2 3 4 6, WICKET, WD, UNDO.
- Ideas for us: colour-coded beads, Quick/Advanced scorer toggle, FOW + extras line.

## 2. PSDRepo — Live Cricket Score & Streaming UI (Bashar Bhuiyan)
- Blue theme, 2 Android screens: LIVE CRICKET match cards (team logos, scores, "CSK won by 13 runs", venue/date),
  and PLAYER & TEAM RANKING (tabs Batsmen/Bowlers/All Rounders/Team; T20/ODI/Test pills; rank, photo, name, points).
- Ideas: results list cards, rankings screen with format pills.

## 3. Magnific — sports lower thirds score
- Mostly VS / versus bars: angled plates, centre VS badge, flags either side, metallic edges,
  orange/blue home-away twin bars with 0-0 and half/time, banners "Match day", leaderboard banners.
- Ideas: matches our 12 bar styles; add VS intro bar (pre-match) and flag/crest wings.

## 4. Vecteezy — cricket score board
- Generic: stadium scoreboards, VS shields, group tables (Group A–D, MP W D L Pts), knockout bracket, kit icons, batter silhouettes, LED digit boards.
- Ideas: Points table / group cards, tournament bracket scene, kit icon set (original), LED digit style.

## 5. Aptoide — WicketScore (v1.6.5)
- Live scores, scorecards, ball-by-ball commentary, highlights tab, dark mode, match analysis,
  H2H, Manhattan and worm charts, form analysis, schedules, predictions/polls, notifications, favourites, history.
- Odds/betting features: NOT to be copied (lane rules).

## Not available
Dribbble/Magnific are images; I viewed them but cannot copy designs. Everything we build is original.
